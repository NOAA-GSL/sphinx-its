If you're traveling internationally for work, you are not allowed to bring your NOAA-issued
laptop or mobile device with you. GSL has a number of "loaner" laptops that can be checked
out for foreign travel. Admin services requires at least 2-3 days notice to provide a loaner.
Please plan ahead, and give notice well in advance of your travel.


When you return and have retrieved your data, the computer will be wiped and reformatted.

.. attention::

   This is required by the Department of Commerce (DOC) and NOAA for all foreign travel.

Do's and Don'ts for All NOAA IT Resources
-----------------------------------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :stub-columns: 1
   :align: left

   * - Before you Travel
     - While Traveling
     - Upon Return
   * - Request a loaner laptop and security briefing from Admin Services
     - Assume that all communications are monitored and that your device may be compromised.
     - Return your loaner laptop to for scanning before you connect to a NOAA network or 
       remove any data from the loaner laptop. The loaner laptop will be wiped and reformatted.
   * - Minimize information to only what is required for your work while traveling.
     - Limit use to essential NOAA work functions only. 
     - Do not use your NOAA-issued laptop for personal use.
   * - Review relevant policies complete training. Provide Admin Services with a training
       completion certificate.
     - Report any lost or stolen GFE to the IT department as soon as possible.
     - 
   * - 
     - *Only* connect to the Internet using a GFE cellular device.
     -
   * - Do not bring your regular work computer with you.
     - Do not let any government-furnished equipment out of your control at any time.
     - Do not connect your loaner laptop to any NOAA network nor remove any data before
       you let your IT department scan it
   * - Do not use any personal equipment, kiosk, or other than GFE provided equipment.
     - Do not connect to any untrusted network while in a foreign country.
     -
   * - 
     - Do not use any personally owned devices to access any NOAA network or perform
       any NOAA work.
