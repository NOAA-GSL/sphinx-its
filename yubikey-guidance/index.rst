The `Yubikey <https://docs.rdhpcs.noaa.gov/accounts/yubikey_token.html#yubikey>`_
chapters explain how you can configure and register your Yubikey for use with NOAA systems. 

If you're new to NOAA, here's some useful background information on 
`user accounts <https://docs.rdhpcs.noaa.gov/accounts/accounts_and_projects.html>`_.

.. note::
   This Yubikey is considered Government Furnished Equipment (GFE). It must be safeguarded as
   such. You will be requested to return the key upon closure of your noaa.gov account.
   If you lose your Yubikey you must submit a help ticket to:
   help.ssg.gsl@noaa.gov, or call 720-475-3136
