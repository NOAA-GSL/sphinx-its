CAC
===

The Common Access Card (CAC) is the standard authentication method for NOAA systems. 
To obtain a CAC, work with your local admin services team, as they need to start the
application process. Some labs can issue CACs on-site, otherwise you will have to visit a 
RAPIDS site. The site locator website is `ID Card Office Online <https://idco.dmdc.osd.mil/idco/>`_.

You'll find instructions for
`CAC/PIV Enrollment <https://docs.rdhpcs.noaa.gov/accounts/cac_piv.html#cac-piv-ssh-key-enrollment>`_

.. important::
   If you have a new CAC/PIV you must remove the old CAC/PIV from AIM by visiting the 
   `AIM MFA page <https://aim.rdhpcs.noaa.gov/cgi-bin/mfa.pl>`_.
   Once that is done, use the main AIM page above to register your new CAC/PIV.

DSRC Access
===========

.. attention::
   In case of emergency, dial 911.

The David Scaggs Research Center (DSRC) is a NOAA facility located in Boulder, Colorado.
DSRC is NOAA's building on the campus of Department of Commerce in Boulder. It hosts
CSL, GML, GSL, PSL, SWPC, NWS Boulder, and several other groups. Collectively, the team
at DSRC is known as "NOAA Boulder."

The DSRC is located at 325 Broadway, Boulder, CO, 80305. The building is managed by the Department of Commerce
(DOC) and has its own security and access procedures. Find security and access information on the 
`DOC Boulder Labs website <https://www.boulder.doc.gov/>`_.
